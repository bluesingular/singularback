# Audience research

## ICP definition template

```markdown
# Ideal Customer Profile — <version, date>

## Demographics
- Company size: 
- Industry: 
- Geography: 
- Revenue range: 

## The buyer
- Title / role: 
- Reports to: 
- Team size they manage: 
- Budget authority: yes / influences / no

## Their world
- Primary responsibility they're measured on: 
- Biggest challenge they face day-to-day: 
- What a bad week looks like for them: 
- What a great week looks like: 

## Their buying behaviour
- How they discover solutions: 
- Who influences their decisions: 
- What their evaluation process looks like: 
- Biggest objection to buying: 
- What makes them say yes: 

## In their own words (direct quotes from interviews/reviews)
- Pain: "<quote>"
- Desired outcome: "<quote>"
- Why us vs competitor: "<quote>"
```

## Sources to research
1. Existing customer interviews (ask for access or create interview tasks)
2. Sales call recordings or CRM notes
3. G2 / Capterra / Trustpilot reviews of your product AND competitors
4. Reddit and community forums where your ICP hangs out
5. LinkedIn profiles of best existing customers
6. NPS survey responses

## Synthesising research
Group findings into themes:
- Jobs to be done (what they're trying to accomplish)
- Pains (what's frustrating them now)
- Gains (what a great outcome looks like)
- Triggers (what makes them start looking for a solution)
- Blockers (what stops them from buying)

## Output
Post a structured ICP update as a comment, and create/update `references/icp.md` in this skill folder.
