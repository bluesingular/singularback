# PR outreach

## Outreach types

| Type | When to use |
|---|---|
| News pitch | New product, feature, funding, partnership |
| Expert commentary | You have a POV on an industry trend |
| Data/research pitch | You have original data worth sharing |
| Influencer / creator | Partnership, review, co-content |
| Press release | Formal announcement for broad distribution |

## Finding the right contacts

### For journalist outreach
1. Search for recent articles on your topic: `"<topic>" site:techcrunch.com` (adjust publication)
2. Note the author byline and their recent coverage
3. Find their contact via Twitter/X bio, publication staff page, or Hunter.io if integrated
4. Qualify: Do they cover our beat? Have they written about competitors? Are they active?

### For influencer outreach
1. Search LinkedIn, YouTube, Substack, Twitter for creators covering your space
2. Qualify: audience size, engagement rate, audience-ICP match, content quality
3. Check if they've mentioned competitors or adjacent products

## Pitch email structure

```
Subject: <specific + intriguing — not "press opportunity">

Hi <first name>,

<One sentence showing you've read their work — specific article, not generic praise>

<One sentence: what this is and why it matters to their readers specifically>

<2-3 bullet points of the most compelling facts/angles>

<One sentence CTA — specific ask: interview, product access, data embargo>

Happy to share more — just reply here.

<Your name>
<Title, Company>
<Contact info>
```

**Golden rules:**
- Personalise the first sentence genuinely — generic flattery is worse than none
- Under 150 words total
- One ask only
- No attachments on first contact

## Press release template

```markdown
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE / EMBARGOED UNTIL: <date>

**<Headline — active voice, present tense, newsworthy angle>**
*<Subheadline — supporting detail in one sentence>*

<CITY, Date> — <Company name>, <one-line company description>, today announced <what happened>.

<Quote from company spokesperson — must add perspective, not just repeat the headline>

**About <what was announced>**
<2-3 sentences with key details, benefits, and context>

**Availability / next steps**
<When, where, how much, how to get it>

**About <Company name>**
<2 sentences: what you do, who you serve, one proof point>

**Media contact:**
<Name, email, phone>
```

## Tracking coverage
When coverage is published, post to the task:
```
Coverage secured: <headline linking to article>
Publication: <name>
Reach: <estimated readership if known>
Tone: positive / neutral / mixed
Key quote about us: <if any>
```

## What requires approval before sending
- Any outreach to journalists at tier-1 publications
- Any press release
- Any influencer partnership proposal involving compensation
- Any response to negative press inquiries
