# Brand voice

## Core principles
> These defaults apply to all content. Customise them in your company's
> `references/brand-config.md` file (see below).

| Dimension | Default |
|---|---|
| Tone | Direct, confident, human — never corporate or jargon-heavy |
| Voice | First-person plural ("we", "our") for company content |
| Reading level | Aim for Grade 8 (Hemingway score ≤ 10) |
| Sentence length | Prefer short. Max 25 words per sentence. |
| Paragraph length | Max 3 sentences for digital content |
| Punctuation | Oxford comma. No exclamation marks in B2B copy. |

## Vocabulary guide

### Always use
- Concrete nouns and active verbs
- Specific numbers over vague claims ("3x faster" not "much faster")
- Customer language from actual reviews/interviews

### Never use
- "Leverage", "synergy", "disruptive", "best-in-class", "cutting-edge"
- Passive voice in headlines or CTAs
- Filler openers: "In today's world...", "As we all know..."
- Hedging: "might", "could potentially", "in some cases"

## Content-type templates

### Blog post structure
```
Headline (≤65 chars, keyword-first if SEO)
Subheadline (optional, expands on headline)
Hook (1-2 sentences — the problem or surprising fact)
Body (2-4 H2 sections, each with 2-3 short paragraphs)
CTA (one clear next step)
```

### Social post structure (LinkedIn/X)
```
Hook line (first line must stand alone — it's all they see)
1-3 supporting points (short paragraphs, not bullet lists on LinkedIn)
Closing line with implicit or explicit CTA
Hashtags: max 3, placed at end
```

### Email subject lines
- 40 chars max
- No "Re:", no all-caps
- Question or specific benefit over generic teaser
- A/B test: benefit-first vs curiosity-gap

## Customising for your company
Create a `references/brand-config.md` in this skill folder with:
```
# Brand config
Company name: 
Tagline: 
Core differentiator (one sentence): 
Audience (primary persona): 
Three adjectives that describe our brand: 
Three adjectives we actively avoid: 
Competitor we most want to differentiate from: 
Sample "this sounds like us" sentence: 
Sample "this does NOT sound like us" sentence: 
```

## Self-check before submitting any content
- [ ] Would our best customer recognise this as us?
- [ ] Is every claim specific and verifiable?
- [ ] Is there one clear CTA?
- [ ] Have I removed all jargon and filler?
- [ ] Does the opening hook earn the reader's attention in the first sentence?
